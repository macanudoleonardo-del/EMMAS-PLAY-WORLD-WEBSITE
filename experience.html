Meet Emma

A brave beginning turned into a beautiful contribution.

Emma came into the world early and fragile — a premature-heart survivor. Her spirit wasn't fragile at all. Today she dances, creates and leads, showing every child that a hard start can become something beautiful.

When we choose love, we create harmony.
